4Terms of table authorization

1. Authorization. I authorize Forever Ever (the "Venue") to charge the card presented for the table minimum, all purchases added to this table's check, the 22% show fee, applicable sales tax, and any gratuity I add. I confirm I am the cardholder or am authorized to use this card.

2. Card hold. A pre-authorization hold is placed on my card when I am seated. My bank, not the Venue, controls when unused hold amounts are released, usually within 1–2 business days. Only the final check total is charged.

3. Table minimum. The table minimum shown above is charged in full at seating, whether or not the full amount is consumed. Unused minimum is not refundable, transferable, or redeemable for cash or for another date. Purchases beyond the minimum are charged at menu prices.

4. Deposit. Any deposit paid through SevenRooms is applied to the table minimum and is subject to the cancellation policy in my reservation confirmation.

5. Show fee. A 22% show fee is added to the check. It appears on the receipt as "Show Fee," and is retained by the Venue to cover operating costs. This charge is not a gratuity and will not be distributed to service staff. Sales tax applies to the fee. Gratuity is not included and is at my discretion.

6. Open checks. If my check is still open at closing or I leave without closing it, I authorize the Venue to close it to this card for the full balance shown on the check.

7. Declines. If the hold or final charge is declined, I agree to provide another form of payment before seating or before leaving.

8. Bottles and service. All bottles are served by Venue staff. Unopened bottles may be returned only with manager approval before the check is closed. Opened bottles may not leave the premises.

9. Age and ID. All guests must be 21 or older with valid government ID. The Venue may refuse or end service at its discretion; charges for items already served remain due.

10. Card held. If my card is held with this form, it will be returned when my check is closed. The Venue does not record full card numbers.

11. Disputes. I agree that charges within these Terms are authorized and that I will contact the Venue first with any billing question.

12. Entire agreement. These Terms and my reservation confirmation are the entire agreement for this visit. If any term is unenforceable, the rest remain in effect.
